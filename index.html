What the session covers

  1. Is this an outbreak? Baseline, clusters and pseudo-outbreaks
  2. Case definition and line list
  3. Epi curve: reading the shape, counting back to the exposure
  4. Time, place and person
  5. Attack rates, relative risk and confounding
  6. Lab and kitchen evidence, control from day one, communication and IDSP reporting
  7. The same steps for a NICU cluster, and what to prepare before the next outbreak
The canteen outbreak and all its numbers are illustrative, constructed for teaching. Sources are listed on the last slide.
